## What I Learned Today

### Debounce vs Throttle
- **Debounce**: Waits until user stops action for X ms
- **Throttle**: Executes at most once every X ms
- Both are essential for performance optimization

### TypeScript Utility Types
- `Partial<T>`, `Required<T>`, `Readonly<T>`
- `Pick<T, K>`, `Omit<T, K>`, `Record<K, V>`
- `ReturnType<T>`, `Parameters<T>`
- Makes types more reusable and flexible
